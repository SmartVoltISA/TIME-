# Ω-TIME-033 — Minimality of the positive relational resource

## Status
**STRUCTURAL PASS / FUNDAMENTAL DERIVATION OPEN.**

## Question
Does the causal chain require the specific quadratic resource

`R = 1/2 ||Dq||²`

or do weaker assumptions suffice?

The previous anchor Ω-TIME-032 established that a difference operator can generate
`G = DᵀD ≥ 0`, while strict `G>0` additionally requires complete distinction of the relevant degrees of freedom. It also established that a positive resource plus a common symmetric coupling can imply `ab>0`.

The present test removes the assumption that the resource must be quadratic and asks what is actually necessary.

## Check 1 — Difference alone does not select a scalar resource

Let

`d = Dq`.

Many nonnegative functions can be constructed:

`R₂(d)=1/2 ||d||²`,
`R₄(d)=1/4 ||d||⁴`,
`R_abs(d)=||d||`,
`R_exp(d)=exp(||d||²)-1`.

All satisfy

`R(d) ≥ 0`,
`R(0)=0`.

Therefore the logical implication

`difference → unique quadratic resource`

is **not** established.

### Result
**FAIL for uniqueness.**

Difference establishes distinguishability; it does not by itself select a unique numerical valuation of the distinction.

## Check 2 — What is common to all admissible resource measures?

A minimal candidate resource valuation can be required to satisfy:

1. identity: `R(d)=0` iff the relevant change is indistinguishable;
2. nonnegativity: `R(d)≥0`;
3. locality/additivity for independent channels;
4. continuity under small changes;
5. compositional consistency under coarse-graining.

The first two are compatible with the Ω distinction/boundary chain. Additivity and coarse-graining impose substantially stronger structure.

## Check 3 — Additivity selects a quadratic-like structure under regularity

For independent orthogonal channels, require

`R(x,y)=R(x,0)+R(0,y)`.

If, in addition, the resource is generated by a differentiable norm-like local measure and is invariant under rotations of equivalent channel coordinates, the simplest homogeneous solution is

`R(d) ∝ ||d||^p`.

Additivity of independent squared components is naturally represented by

`R(d)=dᵀGd`.

However, this does **not** prove that Ω must choose `p=2`. A norm with `p≠2` remains nonnegative and can satisfy several weaker requirements.

### Result
**PARTIAL PASS.**

Quadratic structure is strongly compatible with compositional/additive resource accounting, but uniqueness is not derived from difference alone.

## Check 4 — Positive resource is sufficient for the sign mechanism, not the exact quadratic form

The `ab>0` proof only needs a positive definite local channel metric and a common symmetric coupling:

`G>0`,
`S=Sᵀ`,
`A=G⁻¹S`.

The exact microscopic origin of `G` can therefore be broader than `DᵀD`.

For two channels,

`G=diag(g₁,g₂)`, `g₁,g₂>0`,
`S=[[0,s],[s,0]]`,

still gives

`ab=s²/(g₁g₂)>0`.

### Result
**PASS.**

The causal-sign mechanism is robust to the detailed positive resource representation.

## Check 5 — Negative control: nonnegative but non-complete resource

Take

`D=[[1,-1],[1,-1]]`.

Then

`G=DᵀD`

has a null direction. A nonzero state change can therefore carry zero measured resource.

Such a structure cannot define a strictly positive local metric over all relevant degrees of freedom.

### Result
**FAIL for strict positivity.**

Required condition:

`ker(D) ∩ V_relevant = {0}`.

This is better understood as **completeness of distinction**.

## Check 6 — Negative control: arbitrary signed resource

Allow

`G`

to be indefinite.

Then a nonzero resource direction may have negative valuation, and the conservation-compatibility condition no longer forces the two coupling coefficients to have the same sign.

### Result
**FAIL.**

Positivity is not cosmetic; it is structurally active in the derivation of `ab>0`.

## Check 7 — Numerical sweep of the full sign mechanism

For random positive diagonal metrics

`g₁,g₂ ∈ (10⁻³,10³)`

and nonzero symmetric coupling `s`, construct

`G=diag(g₁,g₂)`,
`S=[[0,s],[s,0]]`,
`A=G⁻¹S`.

The exact identities are

`GA=S`,
`ab=s²/(g₁g₂)>0`,
`λ=±|s|/sqrt(g₁g₂)`.

A numerical implementation should therefore show no imaginary characteristic component apart from floating-point error.

### Result
**PASS.**

The result is algebraic and does not depend on fine tuning of `g₁,g₂,s`.

## Check 8 — Conservation does not determine positivity

A conservation law can preserve an indefinite quadratic form as well as a positive one. Therefore

`conservation → positivity`

is not valid in general.

Likewise,

`difference → conservation`

is not valid without a rule specifying how changes are exchanged between relations.

### Result
**FAIL for both direct implications.**

The two properties must meet through an additional compatibility principle.

## Check 9 — Reciprocal exchange as the missing structural bridge

Represent exchange between two channels by a single scalar transfer `J`:

`Δr₁ = -J`,
`Δr₂ = +J`.

Then

`Δ(r₁+r₂)=0`.

The same transfer variable appears with opposite balance signs because it is one relation crossing a boundary, not two independent events.

This gives conservation structurally once a conserved additive quantity exists.

But the existence of an additive conserved quantity is still not derived from distinction alone.

### Result
**PASS as mechanism / OPEN as foundation.**

## Check 10 — Stronger interpretation of boundary

A useful non-geometric interpretation survives all controls:

`boundary = criterion separating admissible from inadmissible transitions`.

Memory can modify the future transition space; this is independently recorded in ORISIK as a persistent modification of future possibilities caused by the past. fileciteturn117file0L8-L17

Thus a boundary need not initially be a spatial surface. It can be a transition constraint.

However, a constraint set alone still does not provide a numerical resource value. A valuation/capacity functional is an additional layer.

### Result
**PASS as structural interpretation / OPEN as numerical derivation.**

## Synthesis
The strongest justified chain is now:

`comparison`
`→ difference`
`→ relation/distinction`
`→ transition boundary`

The key discovery is that **resource valuation and conservation are not the same thing**.

- Boundary defines what transitions are allowed.
- Resource measures the magnitude/cost/capacity associated with allowed change.
- Reciprocal exchange constrains redistribution.
- Compatibility of reciprocal exchange with a positive resource metric produces the hyperbolic sign.
